Spaghetti Os and Weiners

Spaghetti Os and Weiners is a classic comfort food dish that combines the nostalgic flavors of canned spaghetti with savory hot dogs. This simple yet satisfying meal is perfect for a quick lunch or dinner, and it's loved by both kids and adults alike. The combination of the soft, saucy spaghetti and the juicy, flavorful hot dogs creates a delightful culinary experience that brings back fond memories of childhood

Ingredients:

  • 1 can of Spaghetti Os
  • 2 hot dogs, sliced
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Optional: shredded cheese, chopped parsley for garnish

Instructions:

  1. Heat a skillet over medium heat and add the butter or oil.
  2. Add the sliced hot dogs to the skillet and cook until they are browned and heated through, about 5-7 minutes.
  3. While the hot dogs are cooking, open the can of Spaghetti Os and pour them into a saucepan. Heat over medium heat until warmed through, stirring occasionally.
  4. Once the hot dogs are cooked, add them to the saucepan with the Spaghetti Os and stir to combine.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste. If desired, sprinkle shredded cheese on top and garnish with chopped parsley.
  6. Serve hot and enjoy your comforting meal of Spaghetti Os and Weiners!
